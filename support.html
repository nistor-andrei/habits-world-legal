Frequently asked questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the iPhone Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Habits World. You can also tap "Manage subscription" in the App's Settings screen. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid being charged for the next one.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open the App's Settings screen and tap "Restore purchases". Use the same Apple ID you subscribed with.

Where is my data stored?

Your habits and progress are stored on your device. The App has no account and no cloud sync, so deleting the App deletes your habit data. You can export it any time from Settings → Export my data.

I'm not getting reminders

  • Make sure notifications are allowed: iPhone Settings → Notifications → Habits World.
  • Check that the reminder is switched on for the habit and that its time is correct.
  • If your phone is in Silent mode or a Focus mode, notifications may arrive without sound or be held back.

How do I add the Home Screen widget?

Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen, tap the + button, search for Habits World and pick a widget. The widget needs iOS 17 or later.

How do I stop sharing usage data?

In the App, go to Settings and turn off "Share usage data". See our Privacy Policy for details.
