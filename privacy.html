Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026

Habits World ("the App", "we", "our") is a personal habit-tracking app. This policy explains what data the App accesses, how it's used, and who it's shared with.

The short version

Your habit data never leaves your device. Habits World has no account system and no backend server — everything you enter (habits, streaks, progress) is stored locally on your phone using on-device storage (SQLite and local app storage). We cannot see it, and we don't collect it.

Two outside services receive small amounts of anonymous technical data: RevenueCat (to manage your subscription) and PostHog (anonymous product analytics). Both are described below.

Data that stays on your device

  • Habit and progress data. Your habits, the days you completed them, streaks and the buildings you've built are stored entirely on your device. They are never transmitted to us or to any third party.
  • Reminders. If you turn on a reminder, the App asks for permission to send notifications and schedules them on your device. The notifications are created and delivered locally by iOS; the App does not send any notification content to a server. You can use the App without allowing notifications.
  • Home screen widget. The widget shows your progress. The App shares a small summary of your habits with the widget through a storage area that exists only on your device, shared between the App and its widget. Nothing is sent over the internet for this.
  • Data export. If you choose "Export my data" in Settings, the App creates a file on your device and opens the iOS share sheet. Where it goes next is up to you; we never receive it.

Data handled by service providers

  • Purchases (RevenueCat). Subscriptions are processed by the Apple App Store and managed on our behalf by RevenueCat, our subscription infrastructure provider. RevenueCat receives a random, anonymous purchaser ID and the transaction and subscription status needed to validate your subscription. It does not receive your name, email, or payment details, which are handled directly by Apple. RevenueCat processes this data on servers in the United States. See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Apple's privacy policy.
  • Anonymous product analytics (PostHog). We use PostHog, hosted in the European Union, to understand how people move through the App so we can improve it. The App sends specific events only: which onboarding steps are completed, when the subscription screen is shown, dismissed, or leads to a purchase or restore, and similar milestones. Events are tied to a random, anonymous identifier generated on your device — not your name, email, or Apple ID — together with basic device and app information such as the device type, iOS version, app version and language. We have turned off location lookup from IP addresses. We do not send the names of your habits, or anything else you type into the App. See PostHog's privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • We don't require or offer account creation or sign-in.
  • We don't sell your data.
  • We don't use advertising identifiers, and we don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We don't show your habit data to other users — there's no social or shared-content feature in the App.

Your choices, data retention & deletion

  • Delete everything on your device. In the App, go to Settings → Delete all data. This permanently erases your habits, your history and your onboarding progress, and cancels all reminders. Deleting the App from your phone also removes this data.
  • Export a copy first. Settings → Export my data creates a file with your habits and history that you can keep.
  • Analytics and purchase records. Deleting data in the App does not remove anonymous analytics events already sent to PostHog, or the subscription record held by RevenueCat and Apple. These are tied only to a random identifier, not to your name or email, so we usually cannot tell which records are yours. If you would like help with a privacy request, email us at the address below and we will do what we can. You can end a subscription at any time in your iPhone's Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.
  • Anonymous analytics events are retained by PostHog according to its standard retention policy.

Children's privacy

Habits World is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date above. Continued use of the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.
