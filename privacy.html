Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 26, 2026

Habits World ("the App", "we", "our") is a personal habit-tracking app. This policy explains what data the App accesses, how it's used, and who it's shared with.

The short version

Your habit data never leaves your device. Habits World has no account system and no backend server — everything you enter (habits, streaks, progress) is stored locally on your phone using on-device storage (SQLite and local app storage). We cannot see it, and we don't collect it.

Data we access

  • Habit and progress data. Stored entirely on your device. Never transmitted to us or any third party. Deleting the App deletes this data.
  • Calendar & Reminders access. If you enable habit reminders, the App creates entries in your device's Calendar/Reminders on your behalf, using Apple's on-device frameworks. We do not read, collect, or transmit the contents of your calendar.
  • Purchases. Subscriptions are processed by the Apple App Store and managed on our behalf by RevenueCat, our subscription infrastructure provider. RevenueCat receives an anonymous purchaser ID and transaction data needed to validate your subscription — it does not receive your name, email, or payment details, which are handled directly by Apple. See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Apple's privacy policy.
  • Anonymous product analytics. We use PostHog to understand which features are used (e.g. screens viewed, buttons tapped) so we can improve the App. This data is tied to a random, anonymous device identifier — not your name, email, or Apple ID — and is not used to track you across other apps or websites. See PostHog's privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • We don't require or offer account creation or sign-in.
  • We don't sell your data.
  • We don't show your habit data to other users — there's no social or shared-content feature in the App.
  • We don't track you across other companies' apps or websites for advertising purposes.

Data retention & deletion

Because habit data lives only on your device, deleting the App permanently deletes it — there is no server-side copy for us to retain or for you to request deletion of. Anonymous analytics events are retained by PostHog per their standard retention policy.

Children's privacy

Habits World is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date above. Continued use of the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.
